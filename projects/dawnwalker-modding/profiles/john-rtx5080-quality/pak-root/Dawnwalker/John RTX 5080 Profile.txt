John RTX 5080 Quality + Smoothness Profile - revision 1.1

Target hardware:
- NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080, 16 GB VRAM
- AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, 12 cores / 24 threads
- 32 GB system RAM
- 3840x2160 display

This profile is an independent, hardware-specific configuration inspired by the
configuration-based approach used in VynnGfx's Optimized Tweaks TBOD mod:
https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/42

It avoids forcing resolution or visual-quality settings and focuses on texture
and Nanite streaming, PSO caching, asynchronous loading, and CPU scheduling.

Revision 1.1 uses a safer 1792 MB Nanite pool and a larger 2 GB asset-streaming
cache. For the best chance of 120-150 displayed FPS at 4K Ultra, use Full
Screen, DLSS Balanced, DLSS 2x Frame Generation, Unlocked frame mode, and
NVIDIA Reflex On + Boost if available.
